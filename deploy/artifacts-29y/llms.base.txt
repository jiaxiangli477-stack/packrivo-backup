# Packrivo

> Packaging sourcing and project coordination for small jewelry, candle and gift brands. Based in Shenzhen, China. We prepare specifications, compare supplier quotations and review samples. Production feasibility, pricing, MOQ, lead time and sample results require supplier confirmation.

## Key facts

- Minimum order quantity: 500 pieces per design.
- Coordination fee: 8–15%, with the calculation basis agreed in writing before paid work starts.
- Reference prices (stock styles, ex-works China, before logo printing, freight and duties, October 2026): drawer jewelry box US$0.10–0.33 per box; rigid magnetic jewelry box US$0.27–0.98 per box. Collapsible gift boxes and mailers are quoted per specification.
- Typical lead times: stock jewelry boxes ship in 1–15 days; custom printed gift boxes and mailers take about 5–30 days after artwork approval.
- Sample lead time: confirmed per project with the supplier.
- Production lead time: confirmed per project after sample approval.
- Location: Shenzhen, China.
- Service scope: specification preparation, supplier quotation comparison, sample review, production coordination.
- Not a factory: Packrivo coordinates sourcing and does not present itself as a manufacturer.
