How it works

From brief to pallet in four steps

  1. 01BriefSend the pack shot, target quantity and where it ships. We reply with a structure and a price range.
  2. 02SampleA physical sample in your artwork, produced in 5–10 days. Nothing is committed before you hold it.
  3. 03ProduceArtwork goes to plate, the run is scheduled, and we send press sheets before packing.
  4. 04DeliverDDP or FOB, palletised, with photos and the material declaration per order.

Questions

Before you ask

What is the minimum order?
MOQ 500 pieces per design for printed boxes. Unprinted stock ships from 100 pieces.
How much does a custom box cost?
Reference prices run from about $0.27 for a small rigid jewelry box at 500 pieces to $2+ for a large magnetic box at low volume.
How long does production take?
Stock styles ship in 1–15 days. Printed custom orders are scheduled after sample approval, usually 7–30 days.
Do you handle the whole supply chain?
We coordinate sampling, production, inspection and freight with vetted suppliers, and charge an 8–15% sourcing fee on order value.
